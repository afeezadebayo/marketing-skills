# Instagram Organic Growth

Instagram is a visual, mobile-first platform where discovery happens through Reels, Explore and search, and relationships deepen through Stories, comments and direct messages. Instagram's head, Adam Mosseri, has said in public updates that signals such as sends (shares in direct messages), watch time and likes matter for ranking, with sends especially important for reaching new people. [Reported, Instagram statements 2024 to 2025] Brands that post polished product photos and nothing else tend to stall; brands that entertain, teach or show real people grow.

This skill builds Instagram organic programmes: profile, content pillars, Reels, carousels, Stories, Lives, captions, hashtags and search, collaboration posts, community and direct messages, shopping features and measurement.

## Operating principles
1. **Reels for reach, Stories for relationships, carousels for saves.** Use each format for its job.
2. **Make content people want to send.** Useful, funny or relatable posts travel through direct messages.
3. **Hook in the first seconds.** Viewers decide almost instantly whether to keep watching.
4. **Show people and process.** Faces, hands, behind the scenes and customers beat static product shots.
5. **Talk back.** Replies to comments and messages build loyalty and signal relevance.

## Formats
| Format | Best for | Tips |
|---|---|---|
| Reels | Discovery and reach | Hook in first seconds, captions, trending or original audio, 15 to 60 seconds often works |
| Carousels | Education, step-by-step, saves | Strong first slide, one idea per slide, end with a takeaway |
| Stories | Daily connection, polls, questions, links | Behind the scenes, interactive stickers, highlights for evergreen topics |
| Lives | Q&A, launches, collaborations | Announce in advance; save replays |
| Single image | Announcements, quotes | Use sparingly for reach |
| Collab posts | Partnerships with creators or brands | Appear on both profiles; share credit |
| Broadcast channels | Updates to engaged followers | Exclusive tips, early access |

## When to use, and when not to
Use it for consumer brands, lifestyle, food, fashion, beauty, travel, fitness, creators, local businesses and B2B brands with visual stories or culture.

Don't use it for paid campaigns (the Performance Marketing `meta-ads` skill) or for audiences who aren't active there.

## Inputs to ask for, in one message
1. Goals (reach, community, traffic, sales, hiring).
2. Account analytics for 6 to 12 months.
3. Audience and competitors or peers to benchmark.
4. Visual assets, products and people available to film.
5. Production capacity for video.
6. Shopping or link destinations.

## Workflow

### Step 1. Optimise the profile
Name field with a searchable keyword, clear bio, link page, highlights covering key topics, consistent visuals.

### Step 2. Choose pillars
Three to five themes, such as tutorials, behind the scenes, customer stories, product in use and entertainment.

### Step 3. Plan the format mix
For example three to five Reels, two carousels and daily Stories a week, adjusted to capacity.

### Step 4. Script and film Reels
Hook, value, payoff; captions on screen; vertical; natural light; test both original and trending audio.

### Step 5. Write captions
First line as a hook; useful detail; a question or call to action; keywords for search.

### Step 6. Use search and hashtags sensibly
Keywords in name, bio and captions help search; a few relevant hashtags help categorise content.

### Step 7. Engage
Reply to comments and messages; use Story stickers; collaborate with creators and customers.

### Step 8. Measure
Reach, non-follower reach, watch time, sends, saves, comments, profile visits, follows, link clicks and sales.

## Reel structure
```
0-2s: hook (bold statement, surprising visual, question)
2-20s: value (steps, demonstration, story)
End: payoff and prompt (result, takeaway, "save this" or "send to a friend who...")
On-screen text and captions throughout
```

## Content ideas by pillar
| Pillar | Ideas |
|---|---|
| Tutorials | "3 ways to style one jacket", "15-minute dinner from our box" |
| Behind the scenes | Packing orders, making products, team rituals |
| Customers | Unboxings, before and after, reviews read aloud |
| Entertainment | Relatable situations, trends adapted to the brand |
| Education | Myths versus facts, quick explainers |

## Profile checklist
- Name field includes a searchable keyword (for example "Ama | Skincare for sensitive skin").
- Bio says who you help and what to expect.
- Link page with the most important destinations, tracked.
- Highlights organised by topic (start here, routines, reviews, FAQs).
- Grid shows recognisable visual style without needing perfection.

## Caption formula
```
Hook line that adds to the Reel or carousel
2-4 short lines of useful detail or story
A question or clear next step (save, send, comment, link in bio)
Keywords people would search for
```

## Stories routine
- Morning: behind the scenes or tip.
- Midday: poll, question box or quiz.
- Evening: answer questions, share customer content (with permission), link to new posts.
Add strong Stories to Highlights so new visitors can find them.

## Metrics that matter
| Metric | Why |
|---|---|
| Non-follower reach | Discovery |
| Average watch time and completion | Hook and pacing quality |
| Sends | Shareability, a key signal for reaching new people |
| Saves | Lasting value |
| Profile visits and follows | Audience growth |
| Link clicks and sales | Business value |

## Trial Reels
Where available, trial Reels show content to non-followers first. Use them to test new hooks, formats or topics without affecting what existing followers see, then share winners to the main grid.

## Direct messages
Reply to messages within a working day, use saved replies for common questions, and treat DM conversations as sales and support opportunities. Prompts such as "send this to a friend who..." or "DM us the word GUIDE" can grow sends and conversations, as long as the follow-up delivers what was promised.

## Collaboration posts
Co-author posts with creators, partners or customers so they appear on both profiles. Agree the concept, credit and disclosure, and label paid partnerships as the platform requires.

## Edge cases and judgement calls
| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| B2B brand | Culture, team, customer stories and short explainers |
| Little video capacity | Simple phone-filmed Reels; repurpose Stories |
| Regulated products | Check platform policies and disclosure rules |
| Reach drops | Review hooks and watch time; test new formats and trial Reels |
| Partnerships with creators | Use collab posts and paid partnership labels |

## Worked example
A skincare brand posted polished product photos three times a week and saw declining reach. The new plan used four Reels a week (routines filmed by the founder, ingredient myths, customer before-and-afters with permission), two educational carousels, daily Stories with questions, and collab posts with three dermatology creators. Captions added searchable keywords. Within three months, non-follower reach rose sharply, saves and sends increased, and Instagram became a leading source of new customers in site analytics.

## Common mistakes
- Only polished product photos.
- Weak openings on Reels.
- Ignoring comments and messages.
- Hashtag stuffing instead of clear keywords.
- Measuring likes instead of reach, sends and saves.

## Questions to ask before you plan
- What would your audience send to a friend?
- Who can appear on camera?
- How much video can you produce each week?

## Self-check before you answer
- Is each format used for its job?
- Do Reels hook in the first seconds?
- Are captions and profiles searchable?
- Are sends, saves and non-follower reach tracked?
